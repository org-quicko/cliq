## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy with Docker Compose">
    Pull the compose file, configure your `.env`, and run `docker compose up -d`. Cliq needs PostgreSQL and Redis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first program">
    Sign up as a user, then call `POST /programs` to create an affiliate program with a name, slug, and visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add promoters">
    Invite promoters to your program. Each promoter gets a unique referral link to share with their audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track conversions">
    Send `POST /signups` and `POST /purchases` from your product whenever a referred user takes an action. Cliq calculates commissions automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
